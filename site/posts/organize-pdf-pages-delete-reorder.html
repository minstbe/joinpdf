Delete and Reorder PDF Pages Visually (No Software Needed)

Published on 2026-11-20 · Remove blank or duplicate pages and drag pages into order — entirely in your browser, with no upload.

Got a PDF with blank pages, duplicates, or pages in the wrong order? You do not need desktop software to fix it. This guide shows how to delete and reorder PDF pages visually, in your browser, with no upload.

Quick steps

  1. Open joinpdf.top and scroll to Organize Pages.
  2. Select your PDF. All pages load as thumbnails.
  3. Delete: hover a page and click the × button. The counter shows how many pages remain.
  4. Reorder: drag thumbnails into the order you want.
  5. Click Save PDF to download the cleaned document. Detect blank pages removes empty scanned pages in one click, and Reset pages restores the original document if you change your mind.

Common use cases

  • Removing blank scanner pages or accidental duplicates.
  • Putting a cover page first or moving an appendix to the end.
  • Extracting a subset of pages in a custom order.
  • Preparing a document for printing or submission.

Organize vs Split — which tool?

Organize Pages is best when you want to edit one document in place: delete, drag, save. The Split PDF tool is best when you want to type page ranges like 1,3-5,9, duplicate pages, or add annotations before extracting.

Privacy note

Deleting pages happens entirely on your device — the original never leaves your computer, and the removed pages are not sent anywhere.
