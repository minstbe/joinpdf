How to Fill PDF Forms in Your Browser (and Flatten Them)

Published on 2026-11-21 · Complete fillable PDF forms with normal inputs, then save flattened or editable. Personal data never leaves your device.

Application forms, tax sheets, insurance paperwork — many PDFs arrive with fillable fields. This guide shows how to fill them in your browser and save a finished copy, flattened or still editable, with no upload.

Quick steps

  1. Open joinpdf.top and scroll to Fill & Flatten Forms.
  2. Select the PDF. Every fillable field appears as a normal input: text boxes, checkboxes, dropdowns and radio groups.
  3. Fill them in. Existing values are pre-loaded so you can correct rather than retype.
  4. Leave Flatten checked to bake your answers into the page (nobody can edit them afterwards), or uncheck it to keep the saved PDF fillable.
  5. Click Save PDF.

Flatten or not?

  • Flatten for submissions: the result looks identical in every viewer and cannot be accidentally changed.
  • Keep editable for drafts you will revisit, or forms a colleague must complete further.

What if the PDF has no fields?

Some forms are flat scans with lines to print and write on. Those have no AcroForm fields; the tool tells you so. For those, print-and-sign or the Sign PDF tool are the practical route.

Privacy note

Form data is often personal. Here it never leaves your device — filling and flattening both happen in the browser.
