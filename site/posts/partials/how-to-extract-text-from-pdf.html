Need a clause from a contract, a paragraph from a report, or plain text to paste into an email or an AI chat? This guide shows how to extract text from a PDF for free, without uploading the file.

Quick steps

  1. Open joinpdf.top and scroll to PDF to Text.
  2. Select your PDF. The selectable text of every page appears in a preview box, with page and character counts.
  3. Click Copy Text to put it on your clipboard, or Download .txt to save a plain-text file.

Why is my PDF empty after extraction?

Extraction reads the text layer that PDF viewers use for selection and search. Scanned documents are pictures of pages — they have no text layer, so there is nothing to extract. For scans you need OCR (optical character recognition), which is a different, heavier process.

Does formatting survive?

Line breaks are preserved; columns and tables become sequential text. For legal citations or code snippets, review the output — plain text cannot carry layout.

Privacy note

The text layer is parsed by your own browser. Neither the document nor the extracted text leaves your device.
