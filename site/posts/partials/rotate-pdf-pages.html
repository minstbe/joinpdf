Sideways pages are one of the most common PDF annoyances — usually caused by scanners or phone exports. This guide shows how to rotate PDF pages permanently, for free, without uploading your file.

Quick steps

  1. Open joinpdf.top and scroll to Rotate PDF.
  2. Select your PDF. Every page appears as a thumbnail.
  3. Use the ⟲ / ⟳ buttons under a page to turn it 90° left or right. The badge shows the current rotation.
  4. Need the whole document turned? Use Rotate all left or Rotate all right.
  5. Click Save PDF. The rotation is stored in the file, so every viewer shows it correctly.

Why do pages look rotated in the first place?

PDF pages carry a hidden /Rotate flag. Scanners often store landscape content with a rotation flag instead of true portrait content. Viewers honor the flag, but some editors ignore it — which is why marks can land in the wrong place. Read more in why some PDF pages look rotated.

Does rotating reduce quality?

No. Rotation only changes a page attribute — the content itself is untouched, so text stays selectable and sharp.

Privacy note

Thumbnails are rendered by your own browser and the saved file is built on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
