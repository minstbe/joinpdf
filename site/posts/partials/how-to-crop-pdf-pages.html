Wide margins, scanner borders, or stray headers can make a PDF awkward to read and print. This guide shows how to crop PDF pages visually, for free, with no upload.

Quick steps

  1. Open joinpdf.top and scroll to Crop PDF.
  2. Select your PDF. A preview appears with a blue frame over the page.
  3. Drag the frame to move it, or drag its corner handles to resize. Everything outside the frame is removed.
  4. Leave Apply to all pages checked to crop the whole document with the same frame, or uncheck it to crop only the page you are previewing.
  5. Click Crop PDF to download. Reset frame restores the full page if you change your mind.

Common use cases

  • Trimming white scanner borders so pages fill the screen when reading.
  • Removing headers, footers or hole-punch marks before printing.
  • Focusing a slide export or poster on the content area.

Does cropping delete the hidden content?

Cropping changes the visible page area (the PDF CropBox/MediaBox). Content outside the frame is no longer displayed or printed, but it can still exist inside the file. If you need to permanently remove sensitive content, cropping alone is not enough — avoid sharing cropped files that contain confidential margins.

What about rotated pages?

Pages with a hidden rotation flag are normalized first, so the frame you draw matches exactly what gets cropped — even on landscape scans.

Privacy note

The preview and the crop both run in your browser. Your file is never uploaded.
