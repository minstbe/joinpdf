Signing a PDF should not require printing, scanning, or paying for desktop software. This guide shows how to add a signature to a PDF for free — drawn by hand or uploaded as an image — without uploading your document.

Quick steps

  1. Open joinpdf.top and scroll to Sign PDF.
  2. Select your PDF. A preview of the first page appears.
  3. Create your signature: draw it on the pad with mouse or touch, or switch to Upload an image (a PNG with transparent background works best).
  4. Choose the page and the signature width, then drag the signature to the right spot.
  5. Click Apply Signature to download the signed file.

Draw vs upload — which is better?

  • Draw is fastest for a quick handwritten mark. Use a mouse, trackpad or touchscreen; pick any ink color.
  • Upload gives the cleanest result if you already have a signature image — for example a photo of your signature on white paper, cropped, or a transparent PNG exported from another app.

Does it work on scanned or rotated pages?

Yes. Pages with a hidden rotation flag are normalized before the signature is placed, so your mark always lands exactly where the preview shows it.

Is this a legally binding e-signature?

A drawn or image signature is an electronic signature and is accepted for many everyday documents. For regulated workflows that require certified digital signatures with certificates and audit trails, use a dedicated e-signature service instead.

Privacy note

Your document and your signature never leave your device — everything is processed in the browser.
