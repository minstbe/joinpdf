Upload limits and email attachment caps are the most common reasons a PDF needs to shrink. This guide shows how to compress a PDF for free in your browser — with a safe lossless mode and a stronger mode for image-heavy files.

Quick steps

  1. Open joinpdf.top and scroll to Compress PDF.
  2. Select your PDF. The original size is shown.
  3. Pick a mode: Lossless keeps every pixel and character exactly as-is; Strong also re-encodes embedded JPEG images at a quality you choose.
  4. Click Compress PDF. The result shows exact before/after sizes and the percentage saved.

Which mode should I use?

  • Lossless for contracts, reports and anything text-heavy: it re-compresses streams and removes structural redundancy. Quality is untouched by definition.
  • Strong for scans, photo books and slide exports: most of the weight is in JPEG images, so re-encoding them at 50–70% quality typically cuts the file by half or more while staying visually clean.
  • If a file is already optimized, the tool tells you honestly instead of handing back a same-size copy.

What stays sharp?

Text and vector graphics are never rasterized. In Strong mode only embedded JPEG photos are re-encoded; everything else keeps its original quality.

Privacy note

Compression runs on your device. The optional optimization engine is a WebAssembly build of qpdf that executes locally — your file is never uploaded.
