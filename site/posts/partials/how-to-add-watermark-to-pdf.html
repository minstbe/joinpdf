A watermark marks a document as a draft, confidential, or belonging to you — without blocking the content. This guide shows how to stamp a text watermark on every page of a PDF for free, with no upload and no software install.

Quick steps

  1. Open joinpdf.top and scroll to Watermark PDF.
  2. Select your PDF. A live preview of the first page appears.
  3. Type your watermark text — for example CONFIDENTIAL, DRAFT, or your company name.
  4. Adjust size, opacity, color, angle and layout. The preview updates instantly so you see exactly what exports.
  5. Choose All pages or First page only, then click Add Watermark.

Choosing settings that look professional

  • Opacity 15–30% keeps the mark visible without hurting readability.
  • Diagonal ↗ is the classic look for DRAFT and CONFIDENTIAL stamps.
  • Center layout suits a single bold statement; Tiled (3×3) covers the whole page and is harder to crop out.
  • Light gray is unobtrusive; red signals urgency or restriction.

Does it work on scanned or rotated pages?

Yes. The watermark is drawn on top of the page content, so it works on scans, photos and text pages alike. Pages that carry a hidden rotation flag are normalized automatically, so the watermark always lands at the angle you chose — see why some PDF pages look rotated.

Privacy note

Everything happens in your browser: the preview, the stamping and the download. The document never touches a server, which matters when the content is confidential — the very reason you are watermarking it.
