How to Add Page Numbers to a PDF (With Live Preview)

Published on 2026-11-20 · Number pages in six positions and four formats, with start number, size and margin controls. Free and private.

Page numbers make long documents easier to navigate, cite and print. This guide shows how to add page numbers to a PDF for free — with a live preview and no upload.

Quick steps

  1. Open joinpdf.top and scroll to Add Page Numbers.
  2. Select your PDF. A preview of the first page appears.
  3. Choose a position (bottom or top; left, center or right).
  4. Pick a format: plain numbers, 1 / 10, Page 1, or Page 1 of 10.
  5. Set the starting number, font size and margin — the preview updates instantly.
  6. Click Add Numbers to download the numbered PDF.

Choosing a style

  • Bottom center is the classic choice for reports and manuscripts.
  • Bottom right suits books and bound documents.
  • Page N of M helps readers of printed packets know nothing is missing.
  • 10–12 pt with a 24 pt margin reads well on A4 and Letter.

What about rotated or scanned pages?

Numbers are placed relative to how the page actually displays. Pages with a hidden rotation flag are normalized first, so numbers always appear upright at the edge you chose — even on scans.

Privacy note

The preview and the numbering both run in your browser. Your document is never uploaded.
